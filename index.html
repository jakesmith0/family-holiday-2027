Family holiday 2027

Not a ranking and not a giant cottage database. These are deliberately different ways the holiday could be good. The useful question is whether the house, the place and the week together make you want to go.

Quick taste calibration →

~£1,000 totalExcellent territory if the place is genuinely good.
~£1,500 totalCurrent sweet spot.
~£125 / couple / nightUseful sanity check. Over £2,000 total is basically out.

Different kinds of good

Walk-out coastSea/town on foot; little outings are effortless.
House carries the weekPool/cinema/games/social space make bad weather much less important.
Easy family resortMaximum toddler convenience; minimum planning.
Ridiculous valueA good-enough-or-better house leaves lots of money for the week itself.
We just love itA deliberate place for emotional appeal to beat the checklist.

How to use this without creating family admin

Send individual options into the group chat. A simple ❤️ love / 👍 like / 🤷 maybe / 👎 no is enough. Comments like “house looks grim but I like the idea” are even more useful than scores.

This is a working research shortlist, not booking advice. Always re-check availability, final checkout price, cancellation terms and room setup before paying.
